Getting started

  • Import Photo or Import Video on the home screen opens the system picker; only the items you select are shared with SnapCanvas.
  • New Project starts a blank canvas in any size; Templates gives you ready-made designs to fill with your photos.
  • Projects save automatically. Long-press a project on the home screen to rename, duplicate, favorite or delete it.

Photo editing tips

  • Every photo, text, sticker, shape and drawing is its own layer. Open Layers to reorder, lock, hide or group them.
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom the canvas; drag inside a selected layer to move it, or use the corner handles to resize and rotate.
  • Background Studio removes backgrounds with erase and restore brushes and a magic wand (Pro). Turn on Show erased to see what you removed.
  • For a transparent result, export as PNG with Transparent background on.

Video editing tips

  • Drag the timeline to move the playhead and pinch it to zoom. Tap a clip to trim it with the handles, change speed, adjust volume, crop or add filters.
  • Split cuts the clip under the playhead. Tap the square between two clips to add a transition.
  • Text, Stickers and Draw open the overlay editor on the current frame. Each overlay gets its own row on the timeline where you can set when it appears.
  • Music adds a sound file from the Files app. Songs from Apple Music are copy-protected and can’t be used.

Exporting

Photos export as JPEG or PNG at full resolution; videos export as MP4 in 720p or 1080p, and in 4K when your footage was filmed in 4K. Keep SnapCanvas open while a video exports; iOS pauses video rendering when an app goes to the background. Finished exports also appear under Recent Exports on the home screen.

Troubleshooting

  • “Not enough free storage”. Large videos need space while they import and export. Free up storage in Settings → General → iPhone Storage and try again.
  • “Can’t save to Photos”. Allow adding photos in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → SnapCanvas.
  • A photo or video is missing from a project. If its original file was removed, SnapCanvas shows a banner where you can replace or remove it.
  • The app closed during an edit. Your edits are saved; tap Continue editing on the home screen to pick up where you left off.

SnapCanvas Pro & restoring

SnapCanvas Pro unlocks every filter, font, sticker and template, advanced collages, layer groups, Pro cutout tools, all video transitions, 4K and 60 fps video, larger photo exports, and removes the watermark. It’s available monthly, yearly, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the period; manage or cancel them in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. To restore Pro on a new iPhone, tap Restore Purchases in SnapCanvas Settings while signed in with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Policies

Read the SnapCanvas privacy policy. In short: SnapCanvas collects no data, and everything stays on your iPhone. Purchases are covered by Apple’s standard Terms of Use.
