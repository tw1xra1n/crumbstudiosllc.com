SnapCanvas: Photo & Video Editor

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

The short version: SnapCanvas doesn’t collect any data. Your photos, videos and projects stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What SnapCanvas uses, and why

  • Photos you pick. SnapCanvas uses the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photos and videos you choose. It never browses your library.
  • Saving to Photos (optional). When you save an export, SnapCanvas asks for add-only access, so it can add files to your library but can’t read it.
  • Files (optional). Photos, videos and music you open from the Files app are copied into your project. The originals are never changed.

Where your data lives

Projects, imported media, undo history and recent exports are stored in SnapCanvas’s private storage on your iPhone. All editing, including filters, cutouts and video rendering, happens on the device. Nothing is uploaded to us or to any third party. If you use iCloud or device backups, your projects are included in that backup like any other app data, under Apple’s control.

Deleting a project removes it and its imported media from your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes everything.

Purchases

SnapCanvas Pro (monthly, annual or lifetime) is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes payment; we never see your payment details. SnapCanvas only checks with Apple which purchase is active on your Apple Account.

Children

SnapCanvas doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
