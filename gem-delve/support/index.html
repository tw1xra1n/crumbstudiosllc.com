Troubleshooting

Many problems can be fixed with these steps. Try them in order.

The game won’t open, freezes or crashes
  1. Close Gem Delve completely: swipe up from the bottom of the screen (or double-click the Home button), then swipe the game away.
  2. Open the game again.
  3. Restart your iPhone or iPad.
  4. Make sure you have the latest version of Gem Delve from the App Store.
  5. Make sure your device has the latest version of iOS or iPadOS that it supports (Settings → General → Software Update).
  6. Check that your device has free storage space (Settings → General → iPhone Storage or iPad Storage).
How do I play?

Drag pieces onto the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Some blocks hold gems: clear their line to collect them. In Adventure, collect the gems each level asks for before you run out of pieces. In Classic, play for a high score. Clear lines on back-to-back turns for combos. Stuck? Boosters at the bottom of the screen smash blocks or swap pieces. You can replay the lesson any time from the game’s Settings.

My progress is missing

Gem Delve saves your coins, boosters, levels, skins and streak on your device. Deleting the App removes your saved progress. If your progress is missing without you deleting the App, please do not delete or reinstall it. Contact us first so we can help.

There’s no sound
  • Turn up your device volume using the volume buttons while the game is open.
  • Check that Silent Mode is off. On some devices, silent mode mutes game sound.
  • Check the music and sound options in the game’s Settings.
  • Disconnect Bluetooth headphones or speakers, or check that they are connected correctly.
Performance is slow or the battery drains quickly
  • Close other apps that are running in the background.
  • Restart your device.
  • Turning off Low Power Mode may improve performance, and turning it on may help battery life.

Purchases and free coins

Gem Delve is free to play. Coins can be earned by playing, and optional purchases are available in the Shop.

  • Free coins: tap Free Coins in the Shop to watch a short video for 40 coins, up to five times a day. If the button says No ad, no video is available right now. Try again in a few minutes.
  • Starter Bundle or Golden Pickaxe missing after reinstalling, or on a new device: open the game’s Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used to buy it.
  • Coin packs: coins are added as soon as Apple confirms the purchase. Coins are used up as you spend them and can’t be restored after deleting the App. If a purchase went through but no coins appeared, close and reopen the game, then contact us if they still don’t show up.
  • Ad privacy choices: where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy choices in the game’s Settings.
  • Reminders: to turn chest and streak reminders on or off, go to Settings → Notifications → Gem Delve on your device.

Refunds and purchases: Apple handles App Store payments and refunds. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reporting a problem

If you have any problem with Gem Delve that these steps don’t fix, email us at dev@crumbstudiosllc.com. Please include:

  • your device model (for example, iPhone 15 or iPad Air);
  • your iOS or iPadOS version (Settings → General → About);
  • the Gem Delve version (shown on its App Store page);
  • what you were doing when the problem happened, and what you expected to happen; and
  • screenshots or a screen recording, if possible.

We will reply as soon as we can.
