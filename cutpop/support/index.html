How to play

The basics

Cubes of the same colour stick together into pieces. Different colours are tied together with ropes. Swipe across ropes to cut them: any piece you cut completely free pops, and new cubes drop in from the top. Each swipe uses one move. Free several pieces with one swipe for a combo, and fill the Pop Meter to earn a Mega Blade that cuts with no limit. You can replay the tutorial any time from Settings.

Modes
  • Levels: hand-tuned goals and stars, with a reward chest every 10 levels.
  • Daily Cut: the same board for everyone each day, resetting at midnight UTC.
  • Endless: pops give moves back; see how far you can go.
  • Ranked Arena: climb from Bronze to Legend. Pop refunds shrink as the run goes on, so every run ends.
  • Zen: no moves, no pressure.
I’m stuck on a level

Every level is checked to be beatable. Use a hint, look for swipes that free several pieces at once, and pop the goal colour first. After a couple of tries the game gives you a free Mega Blade, and later extra moves, to help you through.

Troubleshooting

The game won’t open, freezes or crashes
  1. Close CutPop completely and open it again.
  2. Restart your iPhone.
  3. Make sure you have the latest version of CutPop and of iOS.
  4. Check that your iPhone has free storage space.
My progress is missing

CutPop saves on your device and, if iCloud is on, syncs your progress between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Deleting the App removes the copy on that device. If progress is missing, please don’t reinstall: contact us first.

There’s no sound

Turn up the volume while the game is open and check Music and Sound effects in the game’s Settings. CutPop plays sound even when the silent switch is on.

Cuts don’t land where I swipe

Swipe across the ropes between different colours; same colours can’t be split. Turn on the Magnifier in Settings to see exactly what’s under your finger.

Purchases and ads

Restore my purchases

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the game while signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy. Skins, bundles and Remove Ads restore; PopCoin packs are consumable and are added once when bought.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

About ads

Rewarded ads are always your choice. Occasional ads may appear between games, never during the tutorial or while you play. Remove Ads turns those off. You can manage tracking in iOS Settings and ad consent choices in the game’s Settings where available.
