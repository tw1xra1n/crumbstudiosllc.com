1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Crumb Studios LLC (“Crumb Studios,” “we,” “us”) handles information in connection with our iOS game CutPop: Rope Cut Puzzle Game (the “App”). Our website is covered by our main privacy policy.

2. Summary

  • CutPop has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your progress, coins, skins and settings are stored on your device.
  • If you use Game Center and iCloud, Apple handles your scores and syncs your progress between your devices.
  • Google AdMob shows optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between games. Google collects device and advertising information to deliver and measure them. You choose whether to allow tracking.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information the App handles

CutPop stores your level progress, stars, scores, coins, owned skins, missions, achievements, settings and purchase status on your device so the game works between sessions. The App also keeps simple on-device counters of in-game events (for example, how many levels you have started) to help us balance the game; these counters stay on your device and are never sent to us.

The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and it does not use your camera or microphone.

4. Game Center and iCloud

Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the App submits your scores to leaderboards and reports achievements. Apple shows your Game Center name and scores to other players according to your Game Center settings and Apple’s privacy policy. If you allow it, the App shows your Game Center friends’ scores next to yours. You can play without Game Center.

iCloud. If iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account, the App uses Apple’s iCloud key-value storage to keep your progress in sync between your devices. This data is stored by Apple in your iCloud account; we cannot access it.

5. In-app purchases

CutPop offers optional purchases such as cosmetic skins, PopCoin packs, bundles and Remove Ads. Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. Apple handles your payment information; we never receive it. Apple tells the App which products you own so it can unlock them, and gives us sales reports in aggregated or de-identified form.

6. Advertising

CutPop shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example, for extra moves or bonus coins), and occasional full-screen ads between games. Ads never appear during the tutorial or while you are playing a level. Remove Ads turns off the between-game ads; rewarded ads stay optional.

To deliver, measure and protect these ads, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect device and app information (such as device model, operating system, language and app version), your IP address and the approximate location it indicates, advertising interactions (such as which ads were shown or tapped), device identifiers, and crash and performance information about the ad SDK.

Tracking. After the tutorial, the App asks for your permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, Google may use your device’s advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and you still see ads. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App uses Google’s consent tool to ask for your choices before personalized ads are shown. In US states that give you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising, the same tool offers that choice. Where these choices apply, you can change them in the App under Settings → Privacy choices.

Google uses this information under its own policies. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

7. Notifications

If you turn on the optional Daily Cut reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device. No notification data is sent to us. You can turn it off in the App’s Settings or in iOS Settings.

8. Information Apple provides to developers

Through App Store Connect, Apple may give us crash reports and diagnostics from users who have chosen to share analytics with app developers, and App Store reports such as downloads and sales in aggregated or de-identified form. We use this to fix problems and improve the App.

9. Information you give us when you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you choose to include. We use it only to respond to you and fix the problems you report, and we delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

10. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only as described above: with Apple (purchases, Game Center, iCloud) and with Google (advertising), or when required by law.

11. Children’s privacy

CutPop is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your choices and rights

  • Allow or deny tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Change ad consent choices in the App under Settings → Privacy choices (where available).
  • Turn off Game Center or iCloud for the App in iOS Settings.
  • Delete the App to remove the data stored on your device.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Because we do not hold your game data on our own servers, most requests are best handled through Apple or Google, but contact us and we will help.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, where required, tell you in the App or on the App Store.
