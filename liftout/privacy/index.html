Liftout: Background Remover

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

The short version: Liftout doesn’t collect any data. Your photos, videos and cutouts stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What Liftout uses, and why

  • Photos and videos you pick. Liftout uses the system photo picker, which gives the app only the items you choose. It never browses your library.
  • Saving to Photos (optional). When you save an export, Liftout asks for add-only access, so it can add files to your library but can’t read it.
  • Files (optional). Photos and videos you open from the Files app are copied into your project. The originals are never changed.

How backgrounds are removed

Subject detection uses Apple’s on-device Vision framework, and edge refinement runs in Liftout itself on your iPhone’s processor. Your images are never uploaded to us or to any third party for processing.

Where your data lives

Projects (the original photo or video, its cutout mask, your settings and a thumbnail) are stored in Liftout’s private storage on your iPhone. If you use iCloud or device backups, your projects are included in that backup like any other app data, under Apple’s control.

Deleting a project removes it from your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes everything.

Purchases

Liftout Pro (weekly, yearly or lifetime) is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes payment; we never see your payment details. Liftout only checks with Apple which purchase is active on your Apple Account.

Children

Liftout doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
