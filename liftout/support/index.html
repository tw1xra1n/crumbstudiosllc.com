Photos

  • Tap Photo and pick a picture; the background is removed automatically. Long-press the Photo card to import from Files.
  • Refine has Erase and Restore brushes. Pinch to zoom and use two fingers to move around; Edge switches between soft and crisp edges, and Auto returns to the automatic cutout.
  • When a photo has several people or objects, Subjects lets you tap each one to keep or remove it.
  • Resize frames your subject for square listings, 4:5 posts, 9:16 stories or a tight sticker crop. Drag to move and pinch to scale.
  • Hold the eye button to compare with the original.

Videos

  • Tap Video, trim the part you want, choose Anything or People, then Remove Background. Keep Liftout open while it processes.
  • After processing, change backgrounds, outlines and shadows freely — no need to process again. The scissors button lets you re-trim.

Exporting

Photos export as PNG (keeps transparency), JPG or HEIC; save to Photos, share, or copy to paste into another app. Videos export as MP4, as a transparent HEVC video (.mov) for editors like Final Cut Pro, iMovie and CapCut, or on a green screen.

Troubleshooting

  • Part of the subject is missing or extra background remains. Use Refine → Restore or Erase. For busy scenes, the Subjects tool can add or remove whole objects.
  • “Can’t save to Photos”. Allow adding photos in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Liftout.
  • Video processing stopped. iOS pauses heavy work when an app goes to the background. Open the project again and tap Remove Background.

Liftout Pro & restoring

Liftout Pro unlocks full-resolution exports, removes the watermark, allows unlimited video length in 1080p and 4K, transparent and green-screen video, and batch mode. It’s available weekly, yearly (with a free trial for new subscribers), or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the period; manage or cancel them in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. To restore Pro on a new iPhone, tap Restore Purchases in Liftout Settings while signed in with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Policies

Read the Liftout privacy policy. In short: Liftout collects no data, and everything stays on your iPhone. Purchases are covered by Apple’s standard Terms of Use.
