Carrow: Parked Car Finder

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version: Carrow doesn’t collect any data. Everything it records about your parking spot stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What Carrow uses, and why

  • Location (only while using the app). When you save a spot, Carrow stores your car’s GPS position on your iPhone so it can show it on a map. If you set up a Shortcuts automation, Carrow may read your location briefly when the automation runs. Carrow never asks for “Always” location access.
  • Motion & Fitness. Carrow reads your iPhone’s barometer to estimate which floor you parked on, and counts steps and compass heading for a few minutes after you park so it can guide you back. These readings are stored with your spot on your iPhone.
  • Camera (optional). If you photograph a level sign, the photo is stored with your spot on your iPhone. Carrow reads the text in the photo using Apple’s on-device text recognition; the photo is never uploaded.
  • Notifications (optional). Used only for parking meter reminders that you set.

Where your data lives

Your spots, photos, notes and settings are stored in Carrow’s private storage on your iPhone. A small summary (level, section and time) is shared with Carrow’s own widgets on the same device. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. If you use iCloud or device backups, your spots are included in that backup like any other app data, under Apple’s control.

Past spots are deleted automatically after 30 days by default (you can change this in Settings), and you can delete any spot or clear your history at any time. Deleting the app deletes everything.

Maps and directions

When you ask for walking directions or open your car’s location in Apple Maps, the request is handled by Apple’s Maps service under Apple’s privacy policy. We don’t receive it.

Purchases

Carrow Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes payment; we never see your payment details. Carrow only checks with Apple whether Carrow Pro is unlocked.

Children

Carrow doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
